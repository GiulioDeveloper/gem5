Generated from stats.txt, intervals.csv and tasktrace.jsonl by python3 analysis_scripts/tasktrace/window_view.py <outdir>. Each bar is one stats block; its width is the instructions it committed. The dashed line is the metric over the whole window, computed from the stats summed over all blocks (cycle-weighted for averages such as queue occupancy), so it is not the mean of the bars. When blocks are narrower than two pixels, a pixel column shows their mean (solid) and maximum (faint). The CPU strip shows who ran between two context switches, with the kernel-mode share of each slice as a darker lower band; who ran is grouped by process name, launch tree (the topmost ancestor below systemd on the host, or the container's top process) or where it ran, as in trace_analysis.py, and shading uses the group that ran most of each block. Around events: blocks are indexed relative to the block holding each event, and a block holding several events of the kind counts once.
