Generated by python3 analysis_scripts/tasktrace/sweep_compare.py A B [C ...] from each sweep's summary/sweep.view.json (sweep_summary.py). Runs are paired by checkpoint; ratios and differences are against the first sweep (A), checkpoint by checkpoint. The window value of a metric is computed from the stats summed over the window's blocks (as in window.html), so window IPC is not the mean of block IPCs. Log scale leaves out zero and negative values. Slice categories (agentic only): each window is cut at every context switch, and a slice goes to the category of the task switched in. A category's values are over its slices alone: cycles are the slices' simulated time over the clock period (so idle WFI time counts in "Other"), and event counts are shared out from the stats blocks by instruction overlap, so a short slice partly inherits its neighbours' events. A window with under 1M instructions of a category has no value for it. SPEC has no harness and always shows the whole window. Edge blocks: with k = 1, 3 or 5, the k stats blocks on each side of every context switch, the block holding it included (the first and last k of every thread run), are left out and every metric is recomputed over the rest, the switch metrics and time shares excepted. Top-down: the bars split the rename slots (renameWidth per cycle) into retiring, bad speculation, front-end bound and back-end bound, from the bottom (Level 1), or show the parts of one chosen Level 1 category (Level 2) or Level 2 category (Level 3), still as shares of all slots, on an axis fitted to that category. The shares are recomputed from gem5's counters over the shown blocks or slices; the aggregate bars are the plain means of the checkpoints' shares. Switch frequency is switches per millisecond of simulated time; scheduler instructions are those from __schedule to __switch_to, counted for the slice category switched out of.
