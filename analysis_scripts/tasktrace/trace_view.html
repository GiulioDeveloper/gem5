Generated from tasktrace.jsonl by python3 analysis_scripts/tasktrace/trace_view.py <outdir>. Bars show which task the single guest CPU ran between two context switches; the darker band at the bottom of a bar is the share of that time slice spent in kernel mode on the task's behalf (system calls, interrupts, page faults, the scheduler), and the dark end is the scheduler's own path (__schedule to the switch). Colours group processes by name, by launch tree (the topmost ancestor below systemd on the host, or the container's top process) or by where they ran (host or container); kernel threads and the idle task are grey. Labels are read from the trace, not inferred from purpose; see trace_analysis.py. Instruction counts are the global committed count since the restore.
