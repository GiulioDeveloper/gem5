Generated by python3 analysis_scripts/tasktrace/sweep_summary.py. The window value of a metric is computed from the stats summed over the window's blocks (as in window.html), so window IPC is not the mean of block IPCs. CV is the population standard deviation over the window's stats blocks divided by their mean; for context switches it is taken over the per-block switch counts. Log scale leaves out zero and negative values. In a comparison of two sweeps (sweep_compare.py), runs are paired by checkpoint; the change is B / A − 1 and the difference B − A, of the window values, in percentage points for metrics given in %; log scale applies only to the A and B views. In a comparison of three or more sweeps, each panel shows SPEC on the left and agentic on the right, one violin per sweep (lettered as in the legend); changes are against the first sweep (A), checkpoint by checkpoint.
