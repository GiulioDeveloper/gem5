A: the geometric mean of the baseline's window values. Other sweeps: the geometric mean of each checkpoint's ratio to A, shown as a change against A (geomean ratio − 1; for IPC, the speedup: 1.075× is +7.5%). A checkpoint where either value is zero has no ratio and is left out of that mean (marked "excl. n"); a mean over fewer than half the checkpoints is not shown. Leaving zeros out understates a reduction to zero (e.g. BTB-miss mispredicts with the infinite BTB on SPEC).

Checkpoints

Generated by python3 analysis_scripts/tasktrace/sweep_summary.py. The window value of a metric is computed from the stats summed over the window's blocks (as in window.html), so window IPC is not the mean of block IPCs. CV is the population standard deviation over the window's stats blocks divided by their mean; for context switches it is taken over the per-block switch counts. Log scale leaves out zero and negative values. In a comparison of two sweeps (sweep_compare.py), runs are paired by checkpoint; the change is B / A − 1 and the difference B − A, of the window values, in percentage points for metrics given in %; log scale applies only to the A and B views. In a comparison of three or more sweeps, each panel shows SPEC on the left and agentic on the right, one violin per sweep (lettered as in the legend); ratios and differences are against the first sweep (A), checkpoint by checkpoint, and the summary tables give their geometric means. Slice categories (agentic only): each window is cut at every context switch, and a slice goes to the category of the task switched in. A category's values are over its slices alone: cycles are the slices' simulated time over the clock period (so idle WFI time counts in "Other"), and event counts are shared out from the 200K-instruction stats blocks by instruction overlap, so a short slice partly inherits its neighbours' events. A window with under 1M instructions of a category has no value for it; CV is not computed per category. SPEC has no harness and always shows the whole window. Edge blocks: with k = 1, 3 or 5, the k stats blocks on each side of every context switch, the block holding it included (the first and last k of every thread run), are left out and every metric is recomputed over the rest, the switch metrics and time shares excepted; comparing against 0 shows the indirect cost of a switch (cold caches, TLBs and predictors) mixed with the different work of the switch-heavy phases. Top-down breakdown: each bar splits a window's rename slots (renameWidth per cycle) into retiring, bad speculation, front-end bound and back-end bound, from the bottom (Level 1), or shows the parts of one chosen Level 1 category (Level 2) or Level 2 category (Level 3), still as shares of all slots, on an axis fitted to the largest such category among the cards. The shares are recomputed from gem5's counters over the shown blocks or slices, so they follow the slice and edge settings like every other metric; the "mean" card is the plain mean of the checkpoints' shares. Switch frequency is switches per millisecond of simulated time; scheduler instructions are those from __schedule to __switch_to, counted for the slice category switched out of.
